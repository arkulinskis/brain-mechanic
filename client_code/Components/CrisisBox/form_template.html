Emergency brake

If you might act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or someone is in danger, call your emergency number now: 911 (US, Canada), 999 (UK), 112 (EU), 000 (Australia), 111 (NZ).

You don’t have to be “bad enough” to call. Crisis lines are free, confidential, and there for exactly this moment.
