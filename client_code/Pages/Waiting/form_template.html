The waiting room: four weeks while you wait for care

On a waiting list for therapy or an assessment? Waiting is hard, and an empty diary makes it harder. Here’s a simple route: one small step at a time, built from tools already in the shop, ending with a work order for your first appointment.

Work order

If it gets worse while you wait, don’t wait

Call your local emergency number if you might act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or anyone is in danger. Free crisis lines are behind the Emergency brake, any time.

Where this comes from

The route follows advice from these guides for people waiting for care. Every tool has its own evidence on its page.
